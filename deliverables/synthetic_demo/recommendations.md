# Monthly campaign review: January–June 2025

## Decision requested

Approve a controlled budget test that reallocates a small share of spend from weaker observed efficiency toward stronger campaigns, while preserving a holdout or geo test where possible.

## How to read this review

The six-month synthetic dataset contains **$636,001 spend**, **$2,747,929 attributed revenue**, and **29,929 orders**, for **4.32x attributed ROAS** and **$21.25 CPA**. These figures demonstrate the reporting workflow and do not represent a real advertiser.

## Findings to report

The generated campaign scorecard shows:

| Campaign | Spend | Orders | Attributed revenue | ROAS | CPA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Meta Retargeting US | $79,213 | 7,920 | $736,383 | 9.30x | $10.00 |
| Google Brand US | $66,034 | 5,587 | $507,233 | 7.68x | $11.82 |
| Google Shopping US | $114,714 | 6,258 | $603,723 | 5.26x | $18.33 |
| Meta Prospecting UK | $71,461 | 2,313 | $178,920 | 2.50x | $30.90 |
| Meta Prospecting US | $169,308 | 4,898 | $422,481 | 2.50x | $34.57 |
| Google Nonbrand US | $135,272 | 2,953 | $299,189 | 2.21x | $45.81 |

Retargeting and brand search show the highest observed ROAS, while Google Nonbrand has the highest CPA and lowest ROAS. Meta US Prospecting has the largest spend, so its efficiency merits review. These are descriptive comparisons from synthetic data, not causal findings.

## Recommended action

Do not shift the whole budget based on attributed ROAS alone. Verify tracking and order attribution first, then test a modest reallocation (for example, 10% of eligible weekly budget) from lower observed efficiency campaigns toward Shopping or Retargeting, subject to audience capacity and frequency. Keep audience, offer, and landing page changes stable during the test. Evaluate incremental orders and contribution margin if those data become available.

## Success criteria

Agree on a target CPA or contribution-margin threshold before launch. Review spend, attributed orders, ROAS, and order volume weekly; make the final call after the test has enough conversion volume. Record any concurrent promotions or site changes.
