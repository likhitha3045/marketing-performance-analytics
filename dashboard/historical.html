Interpretation guide

Source: UCI Bank Marketing, bank-additional-full.csv; 41,188 historical phone-marketing records from a Portuguese bank. Dataset range is described as May 2008â€“November 2010, but this file has month/day-of-week and omits exact date and year.

Subscription response rate: records with y = yes Ã· all selected records. Attempt band: campaign field is number of contacts made to a person during the current campaign. Prior contact: pdays = 999 indicates no prior contact; poutcome describes previous campaign outcome.

Not available: media spend, ad impressions/clicks, order revenue, profit, unique customer ID, exact time series. Therefore ROAS, CPA, customer LTV, and incrementality cannot be calculated from this source.

Leakage caution: call duration is excluded from these analyses because it is only known after a call and can reveal the outcome. Demographic variables are not used to recommend targeting.
