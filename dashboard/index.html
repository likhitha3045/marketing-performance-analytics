Method and limitations

Simulation: generated portfolio data, not a real advertiser. Attribution: synthetic last touch. ROAS: attributed order revenue ÷ ad spend; revenue is not profit or incremental lift. Customer value: observed revenue in the six-month window, not predictive LTV. Prior period: equal-length interval immediately before selected dates. The synthetic pattern includes seasonality. Comparison is descriptive, not causal.
